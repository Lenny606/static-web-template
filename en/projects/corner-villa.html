Corner Villa

Harmonious connection of modern architecture with the surrounding landscape.

Concept and Vision

Corner Villa represents a unique solution for a corner plot, where the goal was to maximize privacy while opening the space to light.

Every line and every material was chosen with a focus on longevity and aesthetic purity, which is typical for our studio.

Interior and Materials

In the interior, natural materials such as wood and stone predominate, creating a warm contrast to the raw concrete of the exterior.

Large-format windows blur the boundary between indoor and outdoor space, visually increasing the living area.
