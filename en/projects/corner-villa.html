Corner Villa, Central Bohemia

"The house resembles a footbridge resting in a rotated position above a steep northern slope."

Corner Villa represents a unique solution for a corner plot, where the goal was to maximize privacy while opening the space to light. Harmonious connection of modern architecture with the surrounding landscape is a key element of the entire design.

Every line and every material was chosen with a focus on longevity and aesthetic purity, which is typical for our studio. In the interior, natural materials such as wood and stone predominate, creating a warm contrast to the raw concrete of the exterior.
