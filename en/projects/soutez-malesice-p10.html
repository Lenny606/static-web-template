Garden Restaurant Malešice

"Connection with nature in the heart of the city – a pavilion that becomes part of the park and a meeting place."

The design of the restaurant is based on the concept of lightness and transparency, with the building minimally impacting its surroundings.

The circular floor plan and wooden construction create an organic character that naturally complements the mature greenery of the park.
