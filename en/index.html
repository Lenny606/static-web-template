Cabin, Hodruša Hámre, SK

Hamre represents the concept of an open house that draws from its location. Generous glass surfaces offer constant visual contact with nature, while the internal layout ensures maximum functionality.

The apartment in Harrachov is designed with respect for local materials. Wood and stone play a primary role, creating an authentic atmosphere of a mountain home in a modern wrap.

Harrachov Dušek

Family House Říčany

The family house is conceived as a clear, rational structure set into the terrain. The architecture works with reduction, proportion, and accurate definition of the relationship between interior and landscape. Every element has its reason, nothing is extra.

The house resembles an archetypal rural building divided into two calm masses. Simple gables, saddle roofs, and modest materiality connect to the memory of the place and the surrounding landscape.

Family House Slupečná

Apartment Building Pašman, Croatia

The apartment building is designed as a calm horizontal line set into the Mediterranean landscape. The rhythm of loggias and shading creates a variable facade that reacts to the sun, wind, and views of the sea.
